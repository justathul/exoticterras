About Murina Orange

Murina Orange is a solid orange colour form of Cubaris murina. It is hardy, breeds steadily and loves tropical warmth, making it a great first Cubaris for keepers in Kerala.

Murina Orange care guide

  • Keep humidity high (70–80%) with a moist substrate and some ventilation.
  • Use plenty of leaf litter, rotting hardwood and a little sphagnum moss.
  • Feed protein weekly and keep cuttlebone in the box for calcium.
  • They love warmth, so Kerala room temperatures suit them well. Just avoid direct sun.
