About Dwarf White

Dwarf White isopods (Trichorhina tomentosa) are tiny, blind and almost all female, so every one can breed. They love warmth and humidity, which makes them the go-to cleanup crew for dart frog, gecko and tropical vivariums.

Dwarf White care guide

  • Keep the substrate evenly moist, never soggy.
  • Mix in leaf litter and rotting wood; they live mostly underground.
  • Feed lightly. In a vivarium they will eat waste and mould on their own.
  • Their small size means they are safe with almost any reptile or frog.
