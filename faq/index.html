What is your dead on arrival (DOA) policy?

Every live order is covered by our live arrival guarantee. If isopods, springtails or plants arrive dead or in poor condition, send us a clear photo or video on WhatsApp within 2 hours of delivery. We will replace them or refund you, whichever you prefer.

What should I do when my parcel arrives?

Open it soon after delivery and keep the box and packing. If anything looks wrong, take a photo or video straight away, including the packaging, and message us on WhatsApp within 2 hours. Isopods that look still after a cold or hot journey often recover in a few minutes in a shaded spot, so give them a short time before judging.

What does the guarantee not cover?

It covers losses in transit. It does not cover animals that die after you have set them up, parcels that were refused or left uncollected, or delays caused by a wrong address or phone number. Glass terrariums that arrive broken are covered if you send a photo within the same 2 hours.

Which species should a beginner start with?

Powder Orange and Dwarf White. Both breed quickly, handle small mistakes and are great cleaners in a bioactive tank.

How many isopods should I buy to start a colony?

All isopods are priced per piece. For common species like Powder Orange, Dwarf White or Dairy Cow, 10–15 is a good start. For slower Cubaris and Armadillidium, start with at least 5–6. We send mixed sizes, mostly juveniles and sub-adults, which settle in and breed sooner.

What are isopods and why keep them?

Isopods are small land crustaceans that eat decaying leaves, wood and mould. Keepers use them as a natural cleanup crew in bioactive terrariums and vivariums, and many colourful species like Rubber Ducky and Dairy Cow are kept as display pets in their own right.

Yes. Temperate white springtails are in the shop, and the Beginner isopod pack includes a springtail culture with Powder Orange isopods and food. Springtails handle mould, isopods handle leaf litter.
