Best isopods for beginners in India

Most isopods are easy once the enclosure is right, but a few species forgive mistakes far better than others. If this is your first colony, choose from the list below and leave the rare species for later.

1. Powder Orange (Porcellionides pruinosus)

The best first isopod. They are hardy, breed very fast, tolerate a wide range of moisture and eat almost anything. They are small, about 1 cm, so they suit a cleanup crew in tropical or humid tanks as much as a colony box.

2. Dairy Cow (Porcellio laevis)

Large, active and boldly patterned in black and white. They are easy to keep and breed well, but they are fast and tend to be wriggly, and they like a drier corner and regular protein.

3. Orange Dalmatian (Porcellio laevis ‘Orange Dalmatian’)

The same easy care as Dairy Cow, with orange and spotted patterns. A good choice if you want something to watch in a display setup.

4. Dwarf White (Trichorhina tomentosa)

Tiny, pale and extremely fast breeders. They prefer high humidity and are excellent in tropical vivariums, where they clean up while staying mostly out of sight.

5. Easy Cubaris: Murina Papaya, Glacier and Murina Orange

These are popular for their look and are easy as long as you keep the substrate damp. Breeding is moderate, so they are less suited to quick colony building than Powder Orange, but they are a good step up once you have kept something simpler.

What to avoid at first

Rubber Ducky and similar rare Cubaris need very high humidity, stable conditions and patience. Slow breeders such as Gestroi are fine for a keeper with some experience, but a beginner will probably enjoy faster species more.
