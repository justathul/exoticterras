How to use

  • Add the clay balls (LECA) to the bottom of the jar, then lay the mesh on top so soil can’t fall through.
  • Sprinkle a thin layer of activated charcoal, then add 4–6 cm of moist substrate.
  • Place the wood, then plant the fittonia and fern, pressing the soil firmly around the roots.
  • Cover bare soil with the moss, add the springtails, mist lightly and close the lid.
  • Keep in bright, indirect light (never direct sun). Open the lid for an hour if the glass stays fogged all day.
