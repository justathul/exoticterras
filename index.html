What if my isopods arrive dead?

Send a photo or video within 2 hours of delivery and we’ll replace them or refund you. That’s our live arrival guarantee.

Which species should a beginner start with?

Powder Orange and Dwarf White. Both breed quickly, handle small mistakes and are great cleaners in a bioactive tank.

Each listing shows the count. Colonies are mixed sizes, mostly juveniles and sub-adults, which settle in and breed sooner.
