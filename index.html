Exotic Terras is a small isopod breeder in Ottapalam, Kerala. We keep and breed every colony ourselves, from easy beginner species like Powder Orange and Dwarf White to collector favourites like Rubber Ducky, Gestroi and Cubaris murina morphs such as Papaya, Glacier and Orange. Our isopods and springtails work as a natural cleanup crew in bioactive terrariums, paludariums and reptile or dart frog vivariums, and they are fascinating pets on their own.

Alongside live animals we make ready-to-display mini terrariums and terrarium starter kits, so you can build a living ecosystem at home. Every order ships live across India with our arrival guarantee, and you can ask us anything about care on WhatsApp before you buy.

Which species should a beginner start with?

Powder Orange and Dwarf White. Both breed quickly, handle small mistakes and are great cleaners in a bioactive tank.

How many isopods should I buy to start a colony?

All isopods are priced per piece. For common species like Powder Orange, Dwarf White or Dairy Cow, 10–15 is a good start. For slower Cubaris and Armadillidium, start with at least 5–6. We send mixed sizes, mostly juveniles and sub-adults, which settle in and breed sooner.

What are isopods and why keep them?

Isopods are small land crustaceans that eat decaying leaves, wood and mould. Keepers use them as a natural cleanup crew in bioactive terrariums and vivariums, and many colourful species like Rubber Ducky and Dairy Cow are kept as display pets in their own right.
